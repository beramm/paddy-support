Paddy: Patch Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Paddy is a companion game, not therapy or medical treatment. It is designed to keep camera and gaze information on the iPad and has no account system, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Paddy: Patch Buddy (“Paddy,” “we,” “us,” or “the app”) handles information when the app is used on an iPad. It also explains what happens if you contact support by email.

Camera and gaze processing

Paddy requests access to the front camera to estimate where the player is looking, guide calibration, and support gaze-controlled play.

  • Camera frames, face and eye geometry, calibration observations, gaze estimates, and gaze paths are processed on the device.
  • This information is held only temporarily while the relevant feature is running.
  • Paddy does not save, log, upload, sell, or share camera frames, face or eye geometry, calibration samples, gaze samples, or gaze paths.

Information stored on the device

Paddy stores limited app information locally so settings and gameplay can work across sessions. Depending on the feature used, this may include:

  • language, sound, haptic, character, and gameplay preferences;
  • the caregiver-entered patched eye and playing eye;
  • high scores; and
  • Whack-a-Mole session records such as a random session identifier, start and end times, session configuration, active play duration, scores, trial outcomes, and response times.

These records remain in the app’s storage on the device. Paddy does not transmit them to us or to an analytics service. Apple may include app data in a device or iCloud backup according to the user’s Apple backup settings; we do not control or have access to those backups.

Information we collect from the app

We do not collect data from Paddy. The app does not send us personal information, health information, gameplay records, diagnostics, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, camera data, or gaze data.

Paddy does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, crash-reporting services, social-media SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Children’s privacy

Paddy is intended to be used with adult supervision. The app does not ask a child to create an account, provide a name, enter contact details, or communicate with other users. Because Paddy does not transmit app data to us, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Camera permission can be changed at any time in the iPad’s Settings app.
  • Deleting Paddy from the iPad deletes the app’s local data from that device, subject to any device or iCloud backups controlled by the user’s Apple settings.
  • There is no server account or server-side app record for us to access or delete.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, maintain safety, and improve the app. Do not send photos or videos of children, camera frames, gaze information, medical records, or other sensitive information. Email is handled by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers under their own policies.

Intended use

Paddy is a companion game for playtime during a patching routine. It is not therapy, a treatment, a diagnostic tool, or a medical device, and it does not prescribe a patching plan or promise a therapeutic result. Caregivers should follow instructions from the player’s qualified eye-care professional. Locally stored gameplay information must not be interpreted as a diagnosis or a measure of treatment progress.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Paddy’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published. Material changes to data handling will also be reflected in the app and App Store privacy disclosures as appropriate.
