Before playing

  1. Use an iPad with a working front camera.
  2. Allow camera access when Paddy asks for permission.
  3. Place the iPad in landscape and keep the player’s face clearly visible.
  4. Follow the on-screen calibration steps and keep the head reasonably still.
  5. If a patch is part of the player’s care plan, an adult should confirm it is worn as prescribed.

Camera permission

If the camera is unavailable, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera on the iPad and enable access for Paddy. Return to the app and start calibration again.

Calibration tips

  • Use even lighting and avoid strong light directly behind the player.
  • Keep both the camera and the uncovered eye unobstructed.
  • Stay at the distance shown by the app and avoid moving the iPad during play.
  • Recalibrate after the iPad is moved, rotated, or remounted.
  • If tracking is repeatedly lost, pause, reposition, and calibrate again.

Safety and intended use

Paddy is a companion game for playtime during a patching routine. It is not therapy, a treatment, a diagnostic tool, or a medical device, and it does not promise a therapeutic result.

Paddy does not decide whether patching is appropriate, which eye to patch, or how long to patch or play. Follow the plan provided by the player’s ophthalmologist, orthoptist, or other qualified eye-care professional.

Stop playing and rest if the player experiences eye pain, headache, unusual fatigue, nausea, dizziness, or discomfort. Contact the appropriate healthcare professional for medical questions.

Privacy

The front camera and gaze processing run on the iPad. Camera frames and gaze estimates are not saved or uploaded by Paddy. Read the full Privacy Policy.
